At 0.3 m/s drag is approximately 0.05 N and propulsion power approximately 0.02 W. Total draw is hotel-load dominated. At 1.0 m/s drag power rises to ~50 W — endurance collapses. The 0.3 m/s seabed search speed maximises area coverage per Wh. Oblate geometry (Cd = 0.38) per Pebody (2008): 22–31% drag reduction over cylindrical vehicles of equivalent volume.
