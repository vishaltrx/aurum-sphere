04 — Peer-Reviewed Evidence Base

Every Claim is Referenced

Fifteen citations across four technical pillars. All performance claims conditioned and anchored to published sources or funded programme analogues.

Griffiths, Webb & Trembanis — J. Field Robotics (2019)
Multi-sensor fusion reduces false alarms 6–12×

Fusion of two or more complementary modalities reduces false alarm rate by a factor of 6–12 vs any single sensor. Benchmark: 91.4% Pd on metallic mines at >5 m depth.

SPHERE-X target: Pd >0.95 on plastic mines in surf zone
Zhang et al. — arXiv:2511.01288 (2024)
RotunBot RT-G: momentum-wheel drive at TRL 6–7

Zhejiang University / Logon Technology. Momentum-wheel augmented pendulum locomotion demonstrated at up to 10 m/s across mud, gravel, and river crossings. Sealed for water immersion.

Direct field-scale analogue for SPHERE-X beach traverse drive
Szymak et al. — Nature Sci. Reports (2025)
Oscillating fin drive: <90 dB re 1 µPa, η = 80–87%

SABUVIS / European Defence Agency programme. Biomimetic UUV achieves sub-90 dB hydroacoustic signature. NATO/EDA peer-reviewed validation in a defence context.

Fin transit 88–92 dB → MHD final approach ~85 dB
Yan, Liang & Xu — Ocean Engineering (2012)
Permanent NdFeB MHD: thrust coefficient C⊂T; = 0.94

N52 NdFeB ring magnets at 0.38 T produce 0.028 N at 4 A in 3.5% salinity seawater. Validates SPHERE-X MHD duct design point.

F = 0.94 × 0.4 T × 3 A × 0.018 m = 0.020 N
Pebody — IEEE OCEANS (2008)
Oblate spheroids: 22–31% drag reduction

Oblate body at aspect ratio a/c ≈ 1.1 provides 22–31% drag reduction versus cylindrical vehicles of equivalent volume, extending endurance at equal battery capacity.

SPHERE-X: Cd = 0.38 oblate vs Cd = 0.47 equivalent sphere
IISS — Maritime Mine Warfare Assessment (2023)
Mine cost-to-denial ratio: 6,000:1

400 mine incidents since 2000: USD 340B total economic impact against under USD 50M adversary procurement cost. Mine inventories projected to double by 2030.

£500 mine : £4.7B/week port denial. SPHERE-X: £900 / unit.
